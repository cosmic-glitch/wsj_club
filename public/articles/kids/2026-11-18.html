TFK at New York Comic Con

TFK Kid Reporter Shaiyar Dhillon attended New York Comic Con in October. In this article, he shares his experience with TIME for Kids readers.

Every year since 2006, thousands gather at New York City’s Javits Center to take part in one of the world’s largest comic and pop culture events. My family and I have been going to New York Comic Con (NYCC) since I was about seven months old: I wore a Superman onesie. We love to read comics and books, cosplay, and watch our favorite superhero shows and movies. For us, NYCC is an annual family celebration.

Over the years, I’ve cosplayed some of my favorite characters, such as Dipper from Gravity Falls, Hunter from The Owl House, and Miles Morales from Spider-Man. I’ve also cosplayed Percy Jackson from Rick Riordan’s best-selling Percy Jackson series. At this year’s NYCC, I got to meet Riordan, and other authors, as well.

Authors Abound

Randy Ribay is a National Book Award finalist who writes the Avatar: Last Airbender books. When I met him, Ribay told me he got the gig because he was both a writer and a fan. In 2020, he watched the whole series on Netflix and “did one of those long Twitter threads where I watched an episode and then would type up my reactions, my analysis of the episode. And I did that for the entire series. Somebody [saw] that at some point, and they’re like, ‘Oh yeah, this writer is a fan. Let’s ask him.’” Pretty cool. Next up from Ribay is his third Avatar book, about Avatar Sonam. The book was announced at Comic Con.

My family and I spent some time strolling through NYCC’s Artist Alley. I got to meet Kami Garcia, who writes the Teen Titans series. I also met Zeno Robinson, who voices Goh in Pokémon Journeys and Hunter in The Owl House. He signed my Owl House graphic novel.

On Sunday, the last day of Comic Con, I spoke with one of my favorite writers, Rick Riordan. You know him as the creator of the Percy Jackson universe. Riordan was at Comic Con to chat about his new book, The Wild Zone, which came out in September. It’s the first book of the Camp Half-Blood Chronicles. Riordan was joined on a panel by series coauthors Annabelle Oh, Kyle Lukoff, Pablo Cartaya, and Jade Adia.

Oh cowrote the first book in the series. During the panel, she said that main character Katie Kim is “a daughter of Aries, and she’s an underwhelming daughter of Aries. . . . She’s kind of in everyone’s shadow.” Oh added that Katie is “trying to make her way and prove herself”—just like most of us.

Time to Chat

After the panel, I got to sit down with Riordan and Oh and ask them questions about Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone. Here’s part of our conversation.

Shaiyar: Can you tell me about the origins of the Camp Half-Blood series, and how you met the collaborators?

Riordan: I was on set with the Percy Jackson TV show, and we were having conversations about what we could do with the sets between seasons. That got me thinking about if there were stories that we could tell that happened between The Lightning Thief and The Sea of Monsters. I started brainstorming, and I came up with this idea: What if we had four new heroes? And what if I could find four great other authors who could each take one of their voices and bring them to life?

Shaiyar: How does it feel to write in a universe like Percy Jackson? Were you a fan?

Oh: I was. I read them a long time ago, when the first book came out. Rick has such a conversational and approachable voice for readers. It’s like you’re hanging out with a friend and the friend is just telling you a fun story. So I liked his writing style, and I enjoyed his books. When I got the opportunity to work on this, it was an honor. But it was also intimidating, because I know how beloved that world and those characters are. I had to make sure I worked very, very hard to do all of my research on the past material and to make sure I knew enough about the world that I would not let the fans down.

Shaiyar: What do you like better, working on TV or writing books?

Riordan: Writing books. I mean, that’s where I live. No offense to the television industry: They do amazing work. But it’s not my world. I’m lucky that they invited me in and I got to learn more about it. But I am first and foremost always a novelist.

Shaiyar: Is there a scoop you can give us about Season 3 of the show?

Riordan: All I can tell you is that I think it’s the best one yet. You should totally watch it. I think you’ll like it!
